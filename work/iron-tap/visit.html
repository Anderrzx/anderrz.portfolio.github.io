Reservations and to-go orders are handled by phone. Give us a call during business hours and we'll get you set up.

By phone only. Iron Tap can't take reservations or orders by email, and there's no online booking. Please call one of the two lines.

Before you call

  1. Call during open hours

    The phones are answered while the restaurant is open, Tuesday through Sunday. Closed Mondays.

  2. Have the details ready

    Your name, party size, and the day and time you'd like make the call quick.

  3. Line busy? Try the other

    There are only two phone lines, so on busy nights please be patient or try the second number.
