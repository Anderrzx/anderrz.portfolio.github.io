Burger showcase

A scroll-driven breakdown of one burger, layer by layer. Concept art generated with AI, not photos of the real dish.

About this prototype

How it works

  • Scrolling pins the burger; the photo comes apart into labelled ingredients, then stacks back into one burger.
  • Label descriptions are concept copy describing the images, for the owners to confirm.
  • With reduced motion turned on, the burger appears as a still, labeled diagram.
