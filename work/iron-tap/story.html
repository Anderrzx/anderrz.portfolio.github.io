Our story

Built by locals, for Waconia

Iron Tap started with two people who loved craft beer and good food, and a hometown they felt deserved a place to gather.

01 · The founders

Meet Greg & Bria James

Iron Tap is the work of Greg and Bria James. They shared a love of craft beer and good food, and they looked at their hometown of Waconia and saw room for something more.

After years of planning and beer sampling, they took a leap of faith and opened the doors on West Main Street.

02 · The craft

Quality drinks, delicious food

The idea was simple: share a love of quality drinks and delicious food. Today that looks like up to 38 rotating taps and craft cocktails at the bar, and a kitchen turning out house-smoked brisket, pulled pork and ribs.

03 · Hometown

Rooted in Waconia

Iron Tap isn't a chain that picked a spot on a map. It was opened by people from Waconia who wanted their hometown to have this kind of place.

It's a spot for meeting a friend for a drink, or gathering the family for a meal, right in the heart of downtown.
