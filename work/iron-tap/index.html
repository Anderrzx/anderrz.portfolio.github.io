Our story

A hometown leap of faith

Greg and Bria James loved craft beer and good food, and felt their hometown of Waconia needed something more.

After years of planning (and plenty of beer sampling), they opened Iron Tap in July 2015. With the community's support, it has since grown to include The Lounge.
