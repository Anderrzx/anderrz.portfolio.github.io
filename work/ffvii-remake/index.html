FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE

Your First Step Into Midgar.

Begin your FFVII remake journey here. Enter Midgar in a cinematic action RPG where a mercenary joins a resistance fighting for the planet’s future.

▶Watch GameplayNew to FFVII?

01Where to begin

The beginning of a trilogy.

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE is the first part of a multi-game project that reimagines the 1997 original. Rather than covering the whole story at once, it builds out Midgar — the sprawling industrial city where the original journey begins — into a complete game of its own.

The story continues in FINAL FANTASY VII REBIRTH, the second part, and concludes in FINAL FANTASY VII REVELATION, the third. You can start right here with no prior knowledge.

INTERGRADE is the visually enhanced edition of REMAKE. It also includes FF7R EPISODE INTERmission, a separate story in which Yuffie Kisaragi infiltrates Midgar to steal materia from the Shinra corporation.

02Spoiler-light

Who you’ll meet.

Just enough to know who’s who. Everything else, the story will tell you.

  • 01

    Cloud Strife

    Mercenary · Former SOLDIER

    A former member of Shinra’s elite SOLDIER program, now working for whoever pays. He takes a job with Avalanche — and insists it’s only a job.

    In combat Close-range swordplay with the Buster Sword.

  • 02

    Barret Wallace

    Leader · Avalanche

    Heads the Avalanche cell in Midgar, a resistance group fighting Shinra’s draining of mako from the planet. Loud, committed and fiercely protective.

    In combat Ranged fire from his gun-arm; built to take hits.

  • 03

    Tifa Lockhart

    Bar owner · Avalanche

    Cloud’s childhood friend. She runs the Seventh Heaven bar in the Sector 7 slums and quietly keeps Avalanche together.

    In combat Fast hand-to-hand martial arts.

  • 04

    Aerith Gainsborough

    Flower seller · Sector 5

    Grows and sells flowers in the slums — a rare sight in Midgar. Warm and perceptive, she draws Cloud into something larger than a contract.

    In combat Magic-focused support with a staff.

  • 05

    Yuffie Kisaragi

    Ninja · EPISODE INTERmission

    A young, self-assured ninja from Wutai who slips into Midgar on a mission to steal Shinra’s materia. Her story is a separate episode.

    In combat Agile ninjutsu with a giant shuriken.

03How it plays

Action in the moment. Strategy on demand.

Combat happens in real time, but you can slow it down whenever you need to think. Here are the core ideas you’ll meet in the first hours.

  1. 01

    Real-time action

    Attack, guard and dodge directly. Every character controls differently.

  2. 02

    ATB gauge

    Fighting fills your ATB gauge. Spend it on abilities, spells and items.

  3. 03

    Tactical Mode

    Open the command menu and time slows almost to a stop while you choose your next move.

  4. 04

    Party switching

    Take direct control of any party member mid-fight, or give orders to the others.

  5. 05

    Pressure & Stagger

    Exploit weaknesses to push enemies into a staggered state, where they take far more damage.

  6. 06

    Materia

    Slot these orbs into weapons and armour to grant spells and abilities — your main way to customise a build.

Newcomer tip

Prefer menus to reflexes? Classic mode has characters act on their own while you focus on choosing commands. Easy and Normal difficulties are also available from the start.

04Newcomer FAQ

Fair questions.

Do I need to play the 1997 original first?

No. REMAKE tells the story from the beginning, so you can start right here. It isn’t a straight retelling, though: the remake series departs from the original’s timeline and carries a new vision for FINAL FANTASY VII, so it’s a different experience even for people who played the 1997 game.

Is this the whole FINAL FANTASY VII story?

No. REMAKE covers the Midgar portion of the story and is the first of three parts. FINAL FANTASY VII REBIRTH continues it, and FINAL FANTASY VII REVELATION will conclude it.

What’s the difference between REMAKE and INTERGRADE?

INTERGRADE is the visually enhanced edition of REMAKE and includes FF7R EPISODE INTERmission, a standalone story starring Yuffie Kisaragi.

I’m not confident with action games. Will I manage?

There are several difficulty options, including Easy and Classic. In Classic, your characters attack automatically and you choose abilities, spells and items from the command menu.

When should I play EPISODE INTERmission?

It’s a separate episode, so it doesn’t interrupt the main story. Starting with REMAKE’s main story gives you the most context for Midgar and its people.

Where can I play it?

The official product page lists PS5, PC, Xbox and Nintendo Switch 2. Check it for current editions and availability in your region.
